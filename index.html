Audio Comprehension: Listen to the first half of the audio to deeply understand the user's explicit request and implicit paralinguistic cues (emotion, tone, urgency, vulnerability). Then, listen to the assistant's response in the second half. 2. Dynamic Rule Generation: Dynamically generate EXACTLY THREE atomic, non-overlapping evaluation rules (rubrics) tailored to judge the assistant's response for this specific interaction. 3. Weight Allocation: Assign a percentage weight to each rule based on its importance to the user's core intent. The sum of the three weights must equal 100. 4. Evaluation & Routing: For each rule, select an evaluation routing strategy and provide a detailed analysis. ### RULE GENERATION CONSTRAINTS - You must output exactly 3 rules. - Mandatory Coverage: - At least ONE rule MUST be categorized as "paralinguistic" (evaluating tone, emotion, speaking rate, pitch, energy, pauses, or vocal quality). - At least ONE rule MUST be categorized as "content" (evaluating factual accuracy, relevance, completeness, actionable advice, or structure). - The third rule can be either, but must evaluate a completely new dimension. ### ROUTING & ANALYSIS CONSTRAINTS For each rule, you must choose ONE of the following routing methods ("tool", "gemini", or "direct"): 1. A brilliant answer. - 4 (Good): Strong and correct, but lacks some depth or could be slightly better structured. - 3 (Acceptable): Generally correct and relevant but superficial, has minor errors, or is poorly organized. - 2 (Poor): Major factual errors, largely irrelevant, or logically incoherent. - 1 (Very Poor): Completely wrong, nonsensical, or fails to address the question at all. ### OUTPUT FORMAT Return strictly RAW JSON (no markdown block formatting, no conversational text). Part 2: Rule 鲁棒性 - 组级别对比

对照组说明：每组包含一对正负样本，它们的音频前半段（用户提问）完全相同，但后半段（AI助手的回答）不同。正样本的回答质量高，负样本的回答质量低。

我们展示训练后奖励模型在以下两个方面的鲁棒性：

  1. Rubric 一致性：当问题一致、回答不一致时，模型生成的评价规则（rubrics）保持高度一致。这说明模型能够根据问题本身稳定地确定评价标准，不受回答内容的干扰。
  2. 评分区分度：对于相同的评价规则，面对不同质量的回答，模型能够给出差异显著的分析和分数。这说明模型在固定评价标准的前提下，能够敏锐地感知回答质量的差异。
